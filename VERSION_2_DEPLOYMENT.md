## What's New

### 1. A New Dashboard
Your homepage now gives you a clear view of what needs your attention, all in one place:
- **Flags** — items that need your review or action
- **To-Do** — your open tasks at a glance
- **Calendar** — important dates coming up

### 2. Navigation via Hubs
Getting around the app is easier. Related tools and pages are now grouped into **Hubs**, so each Hub gives you quick access to everything for that area instead of hunting through a long menu.

### 3. Workflow Authorization for Time-Off / Holiday Swap Exceptions
Requests that fall outside standard time-off or holiday swap rules now go through a built-in **authorization workflow**, so exceptions get routed to the right person for approval instead of being blocked outright.
