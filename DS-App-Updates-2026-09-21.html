What's new in the DS App

We've made several updates to the DS App over the past week. Here's what changed and what it means for you.

Time Off

Dates now look the same everywhere. Every date in the app displays the same way: day-month-year, like 20-Sep-2026.
More accurate day counts. The app now counts weekends and holidays separately when calculating your time-off days, so your totals are correct no matter which country you're in.
See the full history of a request. Open any time-off request and you'll now see every change made to it — who created, updated, approved, rejected, or cancelled it, and when.

Holiday Swaps

Full history here too. Swap requests now show the same kind of change history as time-off requests.
Supervisors can set up swaps directly. A supervisor can now create a holiday swap for a team member without waiting for the team member to submit one first.

Supervisors and Team Management

Temporary coverage. If a supervisor is going to be out, they can assign someone to temporarily cover their team. Approvals and dashboard access shift to that person automatically for as long as the coverage lasts.
Search by Workday ID. You can now search for a supervisor or team member by Workday ID on the Supervisor Assignments page.
Cleaner lists. Retired team members no longer clutter the Supervisor Assignments list by default. A filter brings them back when you need them.

If anything looks different or you run into an issue, reach out to your BSA team.
