# Top Performers — User Manual

> **Version 1.0 · June 2026**
> This manual is for **regular team members**. It covers nominations and voting. If you manage cycles or review anonymization, see the [Admin Manual](user-manual-top-performers-admin.md).

---

## What is Top Performers?

Top Performers is the program that recognizes outstanding work across the organization. Each award cycle has two phases: a **nominations window** where anyone can put forward a colleague who went above and beyond, and a **voting window** where the whole team ranks their favorites. A committee then reviews the results and announces a winner.

---

## What phase are we in right now?

When you open the Top Performers section you will see a banner with the current cycle name and its active phase. Everything you can or cannot do depends on this phase.

| Phase | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Nominations Open** | Submit a nomination for a colleague |
| **Voting Open** | Rank your top 5 nominees |
| **Awaiting Committee** | View the leaderboard (anonymized) |
| **Results Published** | See the winner and decision |

## Part 1 — Nominations

### 1.1 When can I nominate?

Only during the **Nominations Open** phase. Once the nominations window closes you cannot submit new nominations, so don't wait until the last day.

### 1.2 Who can I nominate?

Any active team member — except yourself. Self-nominations are blocked by the system. You also cannot nominate the same person twice in the same cycle.

### 1.3 Submitting a peer nomination

Navigate to **Top Performers → Nominate** and fill in the form:

**Nominee**
Search and select the colleague you want to nominate. Start typing their name in the search box.

**Achievement** *(required, 80–1 500 characters)*
Describe what they did and why it was exceptional. Be specific — vague praise is much less impactful than a concrete story. You have up to 1 500 characters; use them.

**Your relationship with this person** *(required)*
Select the option that best describes how you work with them:

| Option | When to use it |
|---|---|
| Same team | You work on the same day-to-day team |
| Other team | You collaborate cross-functionally |
| Project | You worked together on a specific project |

**TELUS values alignment** *(optional)*
If the achievement reflects specific TELUS values, check the relevant boxes and add a short description. This is optional but strengthens the nomination.

**Quantitative data** *(optional)*
If you have numbers that back up the achievement (CSAT improvement, calls handled, error rate reduction), include them here. Concrete metrics make a nomination stand out.

Once you are happy with the text, click **Submit**. You will receive a confirmation email.

### 1.4 Draft autosave

The nomination form saves a draft to your browser every 30 seconds. If you close the tab by accident, your progress will be there when you come back — as long as you use the same device and browser. Drafts are stored locally and are not visible to anyone else until you submit.

### 1.5 Submitting a customer nomination (Voice of Customer)

If a customer sent written feedback praising a specific colleague, you can log it as a **Customer Nomination**. This type is marked with a special badge during voting so reviewers know the praise came from outside the organization.

**Customer feedback** *(required, 50+ characters)*
Paste or type the customer's original words as closely as possible.

**Source channel** *(required)*
Where did the feedback come from?

| Channel | Example |
|---|---|
| Email | Forwarded email from a customer |
| Chat | Live chat transcript |
| CSAT / NPS survey | Survey response |
| Call | Phone call transcript |
| Social | Twitter / LinkedIn post |
| Other | Any other written source |

**Feedback date** *(required)*
The date the customer sent the feedback. Cannot be a future date.

**Customer name** *(optional)*
If the customer authorized you to share their name, toggle the switch on and enter it. If they did not, leave this off.

**Supporting files** *(optional)*
You can attach up to 2 files (screenshots, transcripts) to support the nomination.

## Part 2 — Voting

### 2.1 When can I vote?

Only during the **Voting Open** phase. You can vote exactly once per cycle — your ballot is final once submitted, so take your time before clicking Submit.

### 2.2 Who can I vote for?

You can vote for any **approved** nomination in the cycle — except nominations where you are the nominee. The system hides any nomination where you appear as the recipient.

All nomination text has been anonymized at this point: names, specific dates, and identifying details have been removed so your vote is based on the achievement, not on who you know.

### 2.3 How voting works

The voting screen has two panels:

**Left panel — All nominations**
All approved nominations appear here, each showing:
- The anonymized achievement text
- A badge indicating the type: ⭐ Admin nomination, 👤 Peer nomination, ❤️ Customer nomination
- A **✦ Voice of Customer** badge if the nomination came from a customer

**Right panel — My Top 5**
You must fill all five slots. Each slot carries a point value:

| Rank | Points |
|---|---|
| 1st | 10 pts |
| 2nd | 8 pts |
| 3rd | 6 pts |
| 4th | 4 pts |
| 5th | 2 pts |

### 2.4 Placing your votes

You have two ways to add a nomination to your Top 5:

- **Drag and drop** — grab a nomination card from the left panel and drop it into a slot on the right
- **Button** — click **+ Add to my Top 5** on any nomination card and choose a rank from the popover

To reorder your Top 5, drag the slots into a different order. To remove a nomination from a slot, click the **✕** button on that slot.

The **Submit** button only activates once all 5 slots are filled. You will see a confirmation dialog asking you to review your selections before locking in the vote.

### 2.5 After you vote

Once submitted you will see a confirmation message and receive a confirmation email. You cannot change or withdraw your vote. The voting page will show your submitted ballot in read-only mode.

---

## Part 3 — Results

### 3.1 Leaderboard

After the voting window closes, a leaderboard becomes visible showing nominees ranked by their total weighted points. Nominee names remain visible at this stage. You can see:

- Total weighted points each nominee received
- Number of individual voters who included them
- Number of nominations they received
- Distribution of votes by rank (how many people ranked them 1st, 2nd, etc.)

Click any nominee row to open their **Candidate Detail panel**, which shows the full anonymized text of every nomination they received.

### 3.2 Winner announcement

After the committee completes its review, the cycle moves to **Results Published**. The winner is announced along with the committee's written justification for their decision.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I see who voted for whom?**
No. Voting is completely anonymous. No one — including admins — can see which individuals voted for which nominees.

**Can I see who submitted a nomination?**
During voting the text is anonymized and the author's name is not shown. After results are published the original nominations may be viewable depending on your access level.

**What if I realize my vote was wrong?**
Votes are final once submitted. Please review your Top 5 carefully before clicking Submit.

**Can I submit more than one nomination per cycle?**
You can nominate multiple different people in the same cycle, but you cannot nominate the same person twice.

**My draft disappeared — what happened?**
Drafts are stored in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data, switching browsers, or using a different device will lose the draft.
