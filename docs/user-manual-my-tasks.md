# My Tasks — User Manual

> **Version 1.0 · June 2026**
> This manual is for **team members** managing their personal task inbox. If you are an administrator creating or managing tasks across the team, see the [Standalone Tasks Admin Manual](user-manual-standalone-tasks.md).

---

## What is My Tasks?

My Tasks is your personal inbox for work items assigned to you directly by an administrator. Each task represents something specific you need to complete or acknowledge. Tasks stay in your inbox until you act on them — there is no automatic expiry.

Navigate to **Tasks → Standalone** from the main menu to open your inbox.

---

## Your inbox at a glance

The inbox shows only tasks assigned to you that are still **pending** — completed or dismissed tasks do not appear here. Each row shows:

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Title** | What you need to do |
| **Priority** | How urgent it is: `Low`, `Medium`, `High`, or `Critical` |
| **Due Date** | The deadline — shown in **red** if you are past it |

Tasks are sorted with the earliest due date at the top. Tasks without a due date appear at the bottom.

### Filtering

If your inbox has many tasks, use the **Priority** filter to focus on what matters most. Click **Reset** to clear it.

---

## Opening a task

Click any row to open the task drawer. Inside you will find:

- The full title and description
- Priority and due date
- Any comments left by the person who assigned the task or by previous reviewers

Read the description carefully before acting — it usually contains instructions or context you will need.

---

## Adding a comment

You can leave a comment on any task before or after resolving it. Use this to ask a question, log what you did, or explain your decision.

Type your message in the comment box at the bottom of the drawer and click **Add Comment**. Comments are permanent — they cannot be edited or deleted — and the administrator who assigned the task can see them.

---

## Resolving a task

Every task has two resolution options: **Mark Complete** and **Dismiss**.

### Mark Complete

Use this when you have finished the work. Click **Mark Complete** in the task drawer.

A comment is optional — you can describe what you did, but the task will close either way.

### Dismiss

Use this when the task no longer applies — for example, it was resolved another way, the situation changed, or it was assigned to you by mistake.

Dismissing **requires a comment**. Briefly explain why you are not completing it. The administrator will see this when they review resolved tasks.

---

## Recurring tasks

Some tasks are generated automatically on a schedule — for example, a follow-up that repeats every 30 days after each team meeting. You can tell these apart because they show a **Template** label with the template name.

When you mark a recurring task complete, you may be asked to upload a **meeting transcript**. This is how the system knows when the next task should be due.

**If you upload a transcript:**
The system reads the meeting date from the file and schedules the next task automatically. You do not need to do anything else.

**If you skip the transcript:**
The task still closes, but the next instance is **not** scheduled automatically. You will see a warning message when this happens. Let your administrator know so they can create the next task manually if needed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I see tasks that I already completed?**
No — your inbox only shows pending tasks. Once you complete or dismiss a task it disappears from your list. Contact your administrator if you need to review a resolved task.

**What happens if I miss a due date?**
The due date turns red to flag it as overdue, but you can still complete or dismiss the task. Missing a due date does not automatically close or escalate the task.

**Can I reassign a task to someone else?**
No. Only administrators can create and assign tasks. If a task was assigned to you in error, dismiss it with a comment explaining the situation.

**Do I get notified when a new task is assigned?**
Check with your administrator — notification settings vary by deployment.

**What is the difference between a standalone task and a workflow task?**
Workflow tasks are part of multi-step automated processes and appear in the **Workflow** tab. Standalone tasks are direct, one-off assignments and appear in the **Standalone** tab. The steps to complete them are the same, but workflow tasks may have dependencies or require specific inputs defined by the process.
